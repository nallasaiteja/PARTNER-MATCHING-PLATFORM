# Partner Matching Platform — Member User Manual

> **Document Version:** 1.0.0  
> **Platform:** Partner Matching Platform (Telugu Matrimonial Portal)  
> **Audience:** Registered Members, Prospective Brides, Grooms, and Families

---

## 1. Getting Started

### 1.1 Quick Registration & ID Creation
The platform enables rapid registration so families can create an account in under 60 seconds and complete detailed horoscope or family background later:
1. Navigate to the platform homepage.
2. Click **Login / Register** in the top navigation bar.
3. Select the **Quick Registration** tab.
4. Enter your **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Gender**, **Date of Birth**, **Marital Status**, and **Caste**.
5. Select your verification channel (**Mobile SMS OTP** or **Email OTP**).
6. Enter your 10-digit mobile number and click **Send OTP**.
7. Enter the 6-digit verification code received and accept the Terms & Conditions.
8. Click **Complete Quick Registration**.
9. Your unique Member ID (e.g., `M2026000001`) will be issued immediately.

### 1.2 Secure Login
Members can log in through any of three convenient options:
- **Password Login:** Enter your Member ID, Mobile, or Email along with your account password.
- **Mobile OTP Login:** Enter your registered 10-digit mobile number, receive an instant SMS verification code, and log in password-free.
- **Email OTP Login:** Enter your registered email address to receive an instant sign-in code.

## 2. Managing Your Matrimonial Profile

### 2.1 Profile Details & Photograph Upload
1. From your **Member Dashboard**, click **Edit My Profile**.
2. **Personal & Family Details:** Enter information regarding family values, family type (Joint/Nuclear), father's occupation, mother's background, and native place.
3. **Education & Career:** Specify your highest degree (e.g. B.Tech, MS, MBA), institution, current profession, employer, and annual income.
4. **Photographs:** Upload up to 5 high-resolution photographs. Your primary photo will appear on search listings.

### 2.2 Astrological & Horoscope Details
1. Upload your horoscope or Jathakam chart image/PDF under the **Horoscope** tab.
2. Enter your **Rasi**, **Nakshatram**, **Gothram**, and **Padam**.
3. Indicate any specific dosham status (e.g., Kuja Dosham / Manglik).

### 2.3 Privacy & Contact Protection
The platform provides privacy controls to safeguard personal information:
- **Photo Privacy:** Set your photos to visible to all members, or blur photos until you accept an interest request.
- **Phone Number Privacy:** Prevent your contact number from displaying publicly; require other families to send an Interest Request before revealing contact details.
- **Profile Visibility:** Temporarily hide your profile if marriage negotiations are underway.

## 3. Finding Matches & Expressing Interest

### 3.1 Advanced Search
1. Click **Find Matches** in the header.
2. Filter profiles by:
   - **Age Range** (e.g., 24 to 28)
   - **Height**
   - **Caste & Sub-Caste**
   - **Education Level**
   - **Location** (e.g., Hyderabad, Vijayawada, Bangalore, USA)
3. Click **Search** to view matching profiles.

### 3.2 Suggested Profiles
The platform's suggestion engine automatically computes compatibility based on age difference, caste preferences, education alignment, and location proximity:
1. Open your **Dashboard** and view the **Suggested Matches for You** section.
2. Review compatibility scores (e.g., 94% Match) and breakdown reasons.

### 3.3 Sending & Managing Interests
- **Express Interest:** Click **Send Interest** on any profile that catches your attention. You may include a pre-formatted polite greeting.
- **Received Interests:** View pending interest requests under the **Interests** tab.
- **Accept or Decline:** Accepting an interest unlocks contact exchange, allowing families to initiate direct phone calls or WhatsApp conversations.

---

## 4. Packages, Invoices & Payments

1. Navigate to **Membership Plans** to explore available membership packages (Silver, Gold, Platinum).
2. Choose your preferred plan duration and benefits (such as assisted matchmaking or contact revelation limits).
3. Request an official invoice or complete payment through approved branch counters or online payment gateways.
4. Download your official stamped GST invoice from the **My Invoices** section.

---

## 5. Biodata PDF Generation & Sharing

- Click **Download Biodata PDF** from your profile screen.
- A PDF containing your photos, family background, astrological details, and educational achievements will be generated.
- Save and share this PDF directly with family elders and relatives via WhatsApp or email.

---

## 6. Feedback & Complaints

If you encounter any inappropriate profile behavior or technical issues:
1. Click **Support & Complaints** from the footer or dashboard.
2. Submit your concern with the profile ID or incident description.
3. Track the resolution status and administrative responses directly inside your portal.
